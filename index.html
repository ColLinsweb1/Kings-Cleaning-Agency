FAQs
Question? Look here
What is included in a standard residential cleaning?
Our standard cleaning covers all essential upkeep to keep your home pristine. This includes dusting all accessible surfaces, vacuuming and mopping floors, wiping down exterior kitchen appliances, sanitizing countertops, and thoroughly cleaning toilets, showers, and mirrors.
Do I need to be home while your team is cleaning?
No, you do not need to be present! Many of our clients prefer to provide us with a spare key, a lockbox code, or coordinate entry with a concierge. Rest assured, all of our cleaners are fully vetted, background-checked, and highly trained professionals.
Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment?
Yes, we bring everything required to make your space shine—including premium vacuums, mops, microfibers, and specialty cleaning solutions. If you prefer us to use specific products you supply for delicate surfaces, just let us know ahead of time!
Can I customize my cleaning plan?
Absolutely. We understand every property has unique needs. You can easily add specialized add-ons to your appointment, such as interior oven cleaning, interior refrigerator detailing, cabinet organization, or post-construction dust removal.
What is your cancellation or rescheduling policy?
We know life happens! You can reschedule or cancel your appointment free of charge up to 24 hours before your scheduled cleaning slot. Cancellations made within less than 24 hours may incur a small scheduling fee to compensate our team's reserved time.
